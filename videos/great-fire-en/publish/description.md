In September 1666 a fire in a bakery on Pudding Lane burned for four days and destroyed at least 13,200 houses. The Lord Mayor waited, the water wheels under London Bridge burned, and the lead roof of St Paul's melted within half an hour. The Monument to the fire stands 202 feet from the bakery, and it is just as tall.
