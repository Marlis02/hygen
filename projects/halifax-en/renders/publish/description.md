On 6 December 1917 the munitions ship Mont-Blanc caught fire in Halifax harbour and drifted for twenty minutes while the city watched. Train dispatcher Vince Coleman telegraphed a warning and died at his post; at 9:04 the ship exploded, flattening nearly everything within 800 metres and killing at least 1,782 people. Every year since 1971 Nova Scotia has sent Boston a Christmas tree in thanks.
