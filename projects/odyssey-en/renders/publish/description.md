After twenty years away, Odysseus comes home to Ithaca disguised as a beggar, while 108 suitors court his wife. His old dog Argos knows him, his nurse finds the scar from a boar hunt, and he shoots through twelve axes — yet Penelope still fears a god in disguise. The proof is their bed: one of its legs is a living olive tree.
