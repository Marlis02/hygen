On January 15, 1919, a steel tank holding 2.3 million gallons of molasses burst on Commercial Street in Boston's North End. A wave 25 feet high swept through the neighborhood at 35 mph; as it cooled it thickened and trapped people. 21 died and about 150 were injured, and for decades the North End still smelled of molasses on hot summer days.
